## Reliability summary

- **Primary (govt)**: WRD ESDD PDF [2], MWDR Pravah live report [4][9], forest-clearance assessment [10] — highest trust for engineering, storage and biodiversity counts.
- **Secondary (news/wire)**: TOI, ThePrint, Hindustan Times, Deccan Chronicle, ET — good for dates and milestones; figures may round.
- **Tertiary (wiki/posts)**: Wikipedia [1], LinkedIn [6], Facebook [18] — used only where cross-checked by [2]/[4] or news.
- **Known discrepancies**: sanctuary area (341 km² KBA/TOI vs 225 km² Incredible India); command area (1,83,322 ha WRD vs 183,858 ha Wiki); species counts (234/251/300+). Both values recorded where they differ.
